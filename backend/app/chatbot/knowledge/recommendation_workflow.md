# Facility recommendation workflow

The facility recommendation module enables citizens to propose new public civic
facilities in neighborhoods where essential municipal infrastructure is missing,
ensuring urban planning is community-driven.

## Difference between complaints and recommendations

- **Complaints**: Reporting defects, cleanliness failures, or damage on an *existing*
  facility already recorded on the map.
- **Recommendations**: Proposing the construction or installation of a *new* facility
  where none currently exists.

---

## Citizen recommendation submission

Signed-in citizens can submit proposals through two easy ways:

1. **How to access**:
   - **From the Home page**: Scroll down to the *Citizen Recommendation* section and click
     the green **Recommend a Facility** button.
   - **From the Citizen Dashboard**: Click the **Recommend Facility** button (with the
     sparkles icon) in the top action bar.
2. **What to provide**:
   - **Title and description**: Explain the community need and expected public utility.
   - **Facility category**: Propose a Dustbin, Public Toilet, Drinking Water Point, or
     Parking Zone.
   - **Geographic location**: Enter the street address, landmark, and pin the exact
     GPS spot on the interactive map.
   - **Site photograph**: Upload a genuine photo of the proposed street, sidewalk, or
     corner.
   - Click **Submit Recommendation**.
3. **Tracking proposal progress**: On your **Citizen Dashboard**, click **My
   Recommendations** to view the live review status of all your submitted proposals.

---

## Recommendation lifecycle and governance

A citizen recommendation moves through a multi-tier municipal evaluation lifecycle:

1. **`PENDING`**: Newly submitted by a citizen and routed to the Municipal
   Administrator's review queue.
2. **`ALLOCATED`**: The Municipal Admin reviews the proposal, selects an appropriate
   agency field officer, and clicks **Allocate** to commission a field study.
3. **`UNDER_INVESTIGATION`**: The allocated agency officer conducts physical site
   inspections (verifying land ownership, footpath width, drainage access, or traffic
   safety) and records feasibility notes.
4. **`APPROVED`**: The agency approves the recommendation, proposing it for municipal
   budget allocation and installation.
5. **`REJECTED`**: If the site is unfeasible (due to private property restrictions,
   underground utility lines, or traffic hazards), the agency officer records technical
   justification.
6. **`INSTALLED`**: Once construction or placement is completed, the new facility is
   officially registered and published on the live interactive map.
