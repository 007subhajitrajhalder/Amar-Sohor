# Administrator guide

## Administrator responsibilities

Administrators use an `ADMIN` account to oversee platform health, manage
participating civic agencies, allocate citizen proposals, and monitor grievance
resolution quality across Kolkata.

Primary navigation sections in the admin portal:
- **Admin Dashboard**: System-wide metrics (total registered facilities, active
  grievances, resolution rate, and agency performance).
- **User Management**: View citizen and agency accounts, inspect roles, and toggle
  account active statuses.
- **Agency Management**: Create and configure municipal agencies and map handling
  categories.
- **Agency Members**: Add agency personnel and manage member rosters on individual
  agency detail pages.
- **All Complaints**: Global oversight of grievances across all agencies, agency
  reassignments, and workflow overrides.
- **Recommendations**: Review pending citizen proposals and allocate them to designated
  agency officers for field feasibility studies.

---

## Agency and member provisioning

1. **Agencies**: In the admin menu, click **Agency Management** to establish municipal
   agency entities (such as Solid Waste Management, Water Supply Department, Public
   Sanitation Board, or Traffic & Parking Authority) and assign specific facility
   categories (`DUSTBIN`, `TOILET`, `WATER_POINT`, `PARKING`).
2. **Agency personnel**: Click **Add Agency Member** to create `AGENT` user accounts
   linked to specific agencies, granting officers authorized access to that agency's
   assigned facilities and complaints.

---

## Recommendation governance

When citizens propose new civic facilities:
1. Open the **Recommendations** section from the admin menu to view all citizen
   proposals marked `PENDING`.
2. Review the location, proposed category, citizen description, and site photo.
3. Click the **Allocate** button on the proposal card.
4. Select the appropriate agency member from the roster, add guidance notes, and click
   **Confirm Allocation** to dispatch the proposal for an on-site feasibility study.

---

## Static information notice

This assistant uses static project reference guides and can answer aggregate
facility-count questions from public facility records. It has no connection to
administrator sessions, cannot look up user or complaint records, and cannot
trigger administrative actions.
