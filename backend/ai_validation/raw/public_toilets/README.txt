Wikimedia Commons toilet image collection

Source categories:
- Dirty toilets: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Dirty_toilets
- Public urinals: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Public_urinals

Images are stored by source category, matching the project's raw class-folder convention.
The category is a weak source label, not a verified condition label. In particular,
public_urinals does not mean clean, and Dirty toilets may contain non-public settings.
Review labels and public/private context before training a public-toilet condition model.

Each image has per-file author, source, and license details in attribution.csv.
Observe the individual license terms (including ShareAlike where applicable).
This download is not split into train/validation/test and is not wired into the dustbin
training script, which currently expects dustbin-only classes.
